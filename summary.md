# Primetrade.ai Assignment Summary

## Objective
The goal of this project was to analyze the relationship between Bitcoin market sentiment (Fear vs Greed) and trader behavior/performance on Hyperliquid.

---

## Methodology

1. Loaded and cleaned both datasets:
   - Fear & Greed sentiment dataset
   - Hyperliquid historical trader dataset

2. Converted timestamp columns into proper datetime format and aligned both datasets using daily dates.

3. Merged datasets using the common date column.

4. Created key metrics including:
   - Daily PnL per trader
   - Win rate
   - Average trade size
   - Trade frequency
   - Long vs Short ratio

5. Performed comparative analysis between Fear and Greed market conditions using charts and grouped statistics.

---

## Key Insights

1. Traders generated higher average profits during Greed periods compared to Fear periods.

2. Trading activity was lower during Fear periods, indicating reduced market participation and cautious behavior.

3. Frequent traders generally outperformed less active traders during bullish market sentiment.

4. Average trade sizes increased during Greed periods, suggesting higher trader confidence.

---

## Strategy Recommendations

1. Reduce leverage and overall risk exposure during Fear market conditions to minimize potential losses.

2. Momentum-based trading strategies appear more effective during Greed periods due to increased market participation and profitability.

3. Active traders may benefit more during bullish sentiment, while conservative trading behavior may be safer during Fear periods.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates a clear relationship between market sentiment and trader behavior. Sentiment-driven strategies can potentially improve trading performance by adapting risk exposure and trading activity based on Fear and Greed conditions.
